Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Keya Authenticator generates TOTP and HOTP codes for any service that supports standard two-factor authentication.

🔑

Keychain Security

All secrets are stored in the iOS Keychain with the strongest protection class — locked to your device and unavailable when the screen is locked.

🫣

Face ID & Touch ID

Unlock your tokens instantly with biometrics. A 6-digit PIN fallback is always available, with automatic lockout after repeated wrong attempts.

📷

QR Code Scanning

Add any account in seconds by scanning a standard otpauth:// QR code. Works with Google, GitHub, Dropbox, Amazon, Microsoft, and hundreds more.

📦

Import & Export

Import from Google Authenticator, Aegis, 2FAS, and Raivo. Export as QR codes, plaintext, or AES-256-GCM encrypted backup files.

⭐️

Favorites & Reorder

Star your most-used accounts so they float to the top. Drag to rearrange the rest — your order is remembered across launches.

⏱️

Next-Code Preview

Before the current code expires, the next one appears early — so you never have to race the countdown timer.

🌍

6 Languages

Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Latin American Spanish.

⌨️

AutoFill

Fill one-time codes directly from the iOS keyboard in Safari and any other app — no need to switch away from what you're doing. Enable in Settings → Passwords.

🔗

Link-based Setup

Tap any otpauth:// link in Mail, Safari, or another app and Keya Authenticator opens with the Add Token form pre-filled — ready to save in one tap.

Privacy

No data leaves your device.

Keya Authenticator was designed from the ground up to collect nothing. There is no server, no account, and no analytics SDK.

🚫

No Ads

No advertising network, no targeting, no third-party SDKs.

📡

No Network

The app has no network entitlements. It cannot connect to the internet.

☁️

No Cloud

Your secrets never leave your device. No iCloud sync, no backup to any server.

📊

No Analytics

Zero tracking. No crash reporters, no usage analytics, no telemetry.

Proudly open source.

Every line of code is publicly available on GitHub, licensed under GPL v3. Inspect it, audit it, build it yourself. Trust through transparency.
